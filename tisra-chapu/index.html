Aksharas per minute. Spacebar starts and stops.

Hand postures

Rest your right hand on your right thigh (or on your left palm). Tisra chapu uses two closed-hand claps.

Tisra chapu1 + 2

Closed hand

Closed hand: palm facing down, striking flat on the thigh. Between claps, hold the hand still while the other syllables pass.

Each syllable gets a light tick; closed-hand claps on “ta” (1) and “ki” (2), with a deeper accent on samam. On iPhone, turn off silent mode if you hear nothing.
