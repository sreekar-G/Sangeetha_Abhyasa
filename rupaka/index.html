Hand postures for Rupaka

Rest your right hand on your right thigh (or on your left palm). Rupaka can be kept in two ways.

Rupaka, 3 counts

The everyday way to keep Rupaka: clap, clap, wave — “ta ki ta”.

Rupaka, 6 aksharasO 4

The full form: a drutham (clap, wave) followed by a chatusra laghu (clap, then little, ring and middle finger counts).

Sharp clap for claps, soft low tone for the wave, wooden tick for finger counts, a deeper accent on samam, and light ticks for each gathi syllable. On iPhone, turn off silent mode if you hear nothing.
