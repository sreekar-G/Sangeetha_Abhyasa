Hand postures

Rest your right hand on your right thigh (or on your left palm). Every thalam is built from these angas.

AnudruthamU

1 akshara. A single clap: bring the palm down flat on the thigh. No wave follows.

DruthamO

2 aksharas. Clap with the palm down, then turn the hand over so the back of the hand rests on the thigh, palm facing up (veechu).

Chatusra laghuI4

4 aksharas. Clap, then touch the little, ring and middle fingertips down one by one, palm facing down.
